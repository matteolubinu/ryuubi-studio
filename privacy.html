Privacy

What this website collects, and what it doesn't.

Who runs this website

This website is run by Ryuubi Studio, an independent studio based in Italy. For anything about your data, write to quillino.support@gmail.com.

No accounts, no forms

This is a static website. There are no accounts, no newsletter and no contact forms, so I don't collect names or email addresses here.

Analytics, only if you say yes

If you accept in the cookie banner, Google Analytics counts visits and clicks on the buy buttons. It sets cookies (names starting with _ga) and sends data about your visit, such as pages viewed, device type and approximate location, to Google. If you decline, or don't choose, Google Analytics isn't loaded at all. You can change your choice any time from "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page.

Stored in your browser

Your analytics choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.

Fonts

Fonts are hosted on this website. Your browser doesn't contact any third party to load them.

Purchases

Checkout happens on Gumroad. Payment details and the data needed for the purchase are handled by Gumroad under its own privacy policy. This website never sees them.
