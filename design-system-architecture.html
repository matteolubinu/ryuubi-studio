Why I made it

I learned these rules by breaking things

Quillino is my app for people who rent out one or a few properties. Native SwiftUI, data in SwiftData, synced to the user's iCloud with CloudKit. Almost every rule in this pack arrived after a mistake.

  • Sync stalled twice in production, because of two different fields missing from the CloudKit schema. No crash, no visible error: writes were rejected and retried forever.
  • The second time, the cause wasn't what I thought. My test seed wrote files of a few bytes, and real files travel on a second field the production schema had never seen. Read the full story.
  • The sheet header was copied by hand in about 14 places, already drifting in padding and trailing actions. Today it's a single component.
  • A secondary text gray measured 3.6:1 on white. That fails WCAG AA. It's now documented as decorative only.

I wrote it all down so I wouldn't repeat these mistakes on the next app. Then I stripped the private details and made it general.

Part 1 on design systems applies to any platform. Part 2 is written around SwiftData and CloudKit, but the principles (immutable production schema, complete seed, explicit versioning) apply to other managed backends too.

Part 1 works on any platform: the skill shows how tokens map to SwiftUI, UIKit, Jetpack Compose and web, with audit searches for each. Part 2 goes deepest on CloudKit, and has a separate section for schemaless backends like Firestore and for your own API.
