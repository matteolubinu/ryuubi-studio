Refunds

The packs are digital products delivered as an instant download, so all sales are final and there are no refunds. If a file is missing, broken or doesn't match the description, write to quillino.support@gmail.com and it will be fixed.

Why it's written this way

A Markdown file can't be technically protected: once downloaded, it can be copied anywhere. I don't add DRM and I don't pretend it exists. I'm asking you to respect the work behind it, which is what lets me keep publishing more.

What it isn't

The packs contain methods and real cases, not legal, medical, tax or financial advice. The privacy policy template in particular is a starting point to have reviewed by a lawyer before you publish it.
